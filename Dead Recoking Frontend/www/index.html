THE PROBLEM

GPS drops out in tunnels, dense cities, and jammed environments. The old fix — dead reckoning — just guesses forward from your last known speed and direction, and drifts further off course the longer signal stays lost. Our model learns from real sensor data to correct that drift while it's happening, not after.

How this works, in 3 steps
  1. Phone/vehicle sensors (accelerometer + gyroscope) record motion continuously.
  2. A trained neural network turns raw sensor readings into a speed + heading estimate, correcting for sensor bias the way classic physics formulas can't.
  3. An Extended Kalman Filter blends that estimate with GPS when available, and leans on it fully during outages — snapping back to GPS the instant signal returns.
